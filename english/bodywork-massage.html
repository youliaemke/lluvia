Womb Care Ritual:*

The Womb Care Ritual is a nurturing full-body massage combined with an abdominal and womb massage—an invitation to meet your belly and pelvic space with presence, care, and deep listening. Our bodies store far more than physical tension.

Stress, emotional experiences, and significant life events can become held within the tissues and influence our overall well-being. Through conscious breathing, gentle touch, soothing strokes, pressure techniques, and gentle mobilization, the abdominal area is invited to soften and release.

The treatment supports relaxation of the nervous system, encourages healthy circulation, and allows the body to return to a more natural state of balance, connection, and ease. Each Womb Care Ritual is individually tailored to your needs. Depending on what your body is asking for in the moment, the focus may be on deep relaxation, menstrual cycle support, digestive health, scar tissue care, pelvic wellbeing, or emotional regulation. As the abdominal and pelvic space often holds emotional memories and lived experiences, deeper inner processes may naturally unfold during or after a session. These experiences are welcomed with care and without force, allowing your body to move at its own pace.

For deeper physical or emotional patterns, a single session is often just the beginning. Receiving treatments over time gives the tissues and nervous system the opportunity to gradually integrate lasting changes, creating the conditions for restoration, resilience, and the body's own natural capacity to heal. If you'd like to learn more about the background and benefits of abdominal and womb massage, you'll find more information on my blog.

Spiritual Plant Wash:*

A spiritual plant wash is a ritual and sacred act using special plants and herbs to promote mental and energetic cleansing and spiritual renewal. Together we will select special plants that are appropriate for your individual spiritual needs. These plants are not only messengers of nature, but also carriers of wisdom and healing. They give you a warm embrace. Through the combined power of the plants and water, we seal the process of massage. This ritual has a strong purifying effect and it is important to rest or go into mediation afterwards.

Soul Touch Massage

In the hectic confusion of everyday life, this massage is like an oasis of calm and renewal. It's a blend of intuitive Touch & pressure Points, Aromatherapy and Sound. It encourages you to let go of whatever is weighing you down - be it physical pain, emotional stress, or just the pressures of modern life. Sleep problems can be alleviated and it contributes to the overall improvement of health and well-being. In this atmosphere of serenity, you can quiet your thoughts and regain your inner balance. It's a wonderful way to pamper yourself.

Head to Heart Massage

The head to heart massage is a holistic experience that focuses on the deep connection between the head and the heart. This form of massage combines physical touch with spiritual elements to promote mental clarity, emotional harmony and inner balance. You will have time to share your intentions, concerns and expectations before the treatment. This treatment will include the head, ears, neck and chest area. Gentle touch and intuitive acupressure can create relaxation and clarity. These touches aim to promote a sense of opening and letting go. This can help to release emotional tension and stagnation.

In all treatments, I use oils and fragrances (essential oils, homemade aromatherapy sprays) to charm your senses and support the process. If you have any allergies or sensitivities, please let me know beforehand and we will see what we can put together for you.

Included in the price is also a pre - and debriefing and a delicious, warming herbal tea afterwards. 🌿
